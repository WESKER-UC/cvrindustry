About CVR Industries

CVR Industries is a Hyderabad-based manufacturer of CNC machined dies and moulds for refractory, ceramic and forging industries.

With in-house ZICO VMC, conventional milling, drilling and heat treatment support, the company delivers precision tooling with a focus on quality, durability and custom solutions as per drawing.

Core Focus: Quality, Durability, Custom Solutions as per Drawing

Quality Focus

Dimensional accuracy and material suitability are central to every die and mould manufactured.

Durable Tooling

High-wear tool steels and vacuum-hardened components built for long service life in demanding applications.

Custom Solutions as per Drawing

Standard and special profiles manufactured to customer samples and technical drawings.

Product Range

Dies, moulds and tooling components manufactured as per customer drawings and requirements.

A. Refractory Brick Dies
  • Straight Brick Dies

    Standard sizes and custom sizes as per customer drawing, manufactured using high-wear tool steel.

  • Wedge Brick Dies

    Inner wedge, outer wedge and arch brick dies.

  • Key Brick Dies

    Standard key bricks and special key brick profiles.

  • Special Shape Dies

    Anchor brick dies, skew brick dies and bullnose brick dies.

B. Block & Nozzle Moulds
  • Nozzle & Well Block Moulds

    Tundish nozzle moulds, well block moulds and slide gate refractory moulds.

  • Burner Block Moulds

    Round burner blocks, square burner blocks and custom burner block dies.

C. Forging & Industrial Tooling
  • EN19 Forging Dies

    Top blocks and bottom blocks for forging applications.

  • EN353 Dies

    Heavy duty forging application dies.

  • D2 / HCHCr Linears

    Vacuum hardened up to HRC 62–64 for wear resistance.

  • Hydraulic Moulds

    For press applications.

  • Precast MS Moulds

    MS moulds for precast and industrial applications.

  • Plates & Holders

    C45 plates, P20 plates, MS holders and C45 filler blocks.

  • Wear Parts

    HD400 wearing sheets.
